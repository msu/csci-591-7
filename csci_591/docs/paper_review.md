# Paper Review

## Description
Each student will review and present a published, peer-reviewed paper from a conference or journal. Students will be assigned to one of four paper presentation days: A, B, C, or D. Each presentation should be at least 10 minutes and no more than 15 minutes long. Slides should be prepared and used for the presentation.

### Do's
Presentations are required to cover the following from the paper:

- Motivation
- Contributions
- Brief Summary of the Technique or Method
- Brief Summary of Results
- Future Work

Additionally, the presentation should mention:

- The most significant strength of the paper
- The most significant weakness of the paper

Leave at least a few minutes for questions at the end of your presentation.

### Don'ts
For the sake of time, your presentation should leave out these sections from the paper:

- Background
- Related Work

## Submission

Slides should be submitted to Brightspace by end-of-day (AoE timezone) the day before the presentation in PDF format.

## Grading
The assignment grade will be based on:

- Demonstrate technical understanding of material (60%)
- Slides contain all required items (30%)
- Delivery of presentation (10%)
